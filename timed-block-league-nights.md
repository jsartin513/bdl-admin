# Timed-block league nights

**Ask:** Pilot **one 6-team week** — three 20-minute matchups, same opponent for the whole window, two rotates. Keep scoring games inside the block so standings do not change. Revert the next week if it feels worse.

This is a sheet change, not a software change.

---

## What a timed block is

A **block** is a timed window, not a set number of games.

- Both courts run the whole time (two matchups, one clock).
- Those pairings stay put until the block ends.
- Then everyone rotates (5–10 minutes, longer with more teams).
- A 2-hour gym is 10 min setup + 10 min pack-up, leaving **100 min** for games and rotates.
- How many rallies fit in the window is up to the night. It is not on the sheet.

Today a 6-team dual-court night rotates about every 3 minutes (~14 transitions). Timed blocks rotate **twice**.

---

## Recommendation

Do not pick one format for every roster size. Two courts and a 2-hour gym land cleanly on **6 teams** (3 dual-court blocks) and **7 teams** (2 plays each, 4 slots with one court idle first).

| Teams | Recommendation | That night | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **6** | **Pilot timed blocks** | 3 × 30 min, 5 min rotates | Even: every team plays 2, refs 1. Full gym used (10 setup / 10 pack). Cost: meeting everyone takes 3 weeks. |
| **8** | **4-block two-wave**, or week bye | Early 4 then late 4; or 2 sit | 4 blocks: even tonight (2 play / 1 ref / 1 off), 19 min games / 8 min rotates. Rotate waves so groups meet; cover in 4 weeks. Week bye if you would rather run the 6-team sheet. |
| **5** | **Week bye if going long**, or keep today’s short games | 1 sits; 4 play a round-robin | Do not run 2–3 long blocks with a bye each round — uneven, and you lose the full 5-team RR. Sit one team; the other 4 finish a 4-team RR in 3 blocks. Or keep short games so all 5 meet tonight. |
| **7 (2–3 rounds)** | **Week bye** | 1 sits; 6 play even | Only even 2–3 round night. Packed 3-block (5 play 2, 2 play 1) only if nobody can sit. Skip 2 rounds. |
| **7 (4 slots)** | **2 plays each** | 1 court idle, then 3 dual | Even tonight; cover in 3 weeks. Prefer this over packed 3 if a one-court opener is OK. |

**Pilot only 6-team.** Do not roll 5, 7, and 8 onto the same night as a mixed experiment.

## Gym clock

2-hour slot: **10 min setup + 10 min pack-up**. The remaining **100 min** is games plus transitions.

| Rotate | 3 blocks (2 rotates) | 4 blocks (3 rotates) | 5 blocks (4 rotates) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min (6-team, simpler) | **30 min** games | 21 min games | 16 min games |
| 6 min | 29 min | **20 min** | 15 min |
| 8 min (7–8 teams) | **28 min** | **19 min** | 13 min |
| 10 min | 26 min | 17 min | 12 min |

What we actually run:

- **3 blocks, 6 teams:** 30 min games, 5 min rotates. Each team plays 2 = 60 min of play.
- **4 blocks, 7 teams:** 20 min games, 6 min rotates (fewer bodies than 8).
- **4 blocks, 8 teams:** 19 min games, 8 min rotates.

24-minute games do **not** fit 4 blocks once setup/pack are real. 4 × 24 + 3 × 5 = 111, over the 100.

## Week bye (5, 7, and 8)

If the roster is not 6, **do not pack everyone into uneven long blocks**. Sit 1–2 teams for the week and run the even night.

Someone is off the whole week. Everyone else plays the format that already works.

### 5 teams — 1 sits, 4 play

Purple sits this sample week. Dedicated refs. All four play every block. Three blocks **is** the 4-team round-robin.

| Block | Clock | Court 1 | Court 2 | Refs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | 22:00–42:00 | Red vs Green | Blue vs Gold | Dedicated |
| 3 | 44:00–64:00 | Red vs Gold | Blue vs Green | Dedicated |

- Playing tonight: **60 min** each (3 × 20). Or **72 min** if you use 24-minute blocks (~76 min on-court, still under 2 hours).
- Week bye: **0 min**.
- Rotate who sits. Even play in **5 weeks**. All 10 pairs covered in **3 weeks**.

### 7 teams — 2 longer games each (recommended)

Playtime is **already even tonight**. Three weeks is how long it takes to **meet everyone once**, not to equalize minutes.

7 games per night = a 7-cycle (each team plays its two neighbors). K7 splits into 3 such cycles, so the round-robin finishes in 3 weeks exactly.

Two courts cannot run 7 games in 3.5 dual-court blocks. Use **4 slots**: one single-court opener, then three dual-court windows. With 10+10 setup/pack and 6 min rotates, games are **20 min**. Each team **plays 2, refs 1, byes 1**.

### 7 teams — 4 dual-court blocks, rotating extras

If both courts run all four slots (8 games, not 7): **16 play slots ÷ 7**. Two teams play 3, five play 2. Same gym clock as above (20 min games, 6 min rotates).

Red plays 1–2–3 (early extra); Purple plays 2–3–4 (late extra). Only **Gold** has a gap (1 and 4). Zero-gap for all 7 is impossible on 2 courts.

Cover: 8 pairs/night, 21 unique → **3 weeks**.

### 7 teams — everyone here, about 3 plays

**All 7 cannot play exactly 3 in one night.** 7 × 3 = 21 play slots. Two-team games always produce an even number of slots, and two courts add 4 per block, so 21 never lands. Two weeks of that would also fail to even out: same structure both weeks only equalizes 7 teams when each night is already even.

Closest that fits a 2-hour gym: **five 20-minute blocks** (~108 min on-court, 4 rotates, ~12 min left). Six teams play 3, one plays 2.

### 7 teams — 1 sits, 6 play

White sits. The other 6 use the **same 6-team sheet** as the pilot (2 play / 1 ref).

- Playing tonight: **40 min**. Week bye: **0**.
- Rotate who sits. Even play in **7 weeks**.
- A 6-week season cannot split byes evenly (someone byes twice, or one team has an extra play week).

## What we are not recommending

**5 teams, bye each block (2–3 long rounds, everyone at the gym).** Fits 2 hours (3 × 20 ≈ 64 min, 3 × 24 ≈ 76 min; 5 long blocks would overshoot). Play is uneven that night, and you no longer finish a 5-team round-robin in one night. Use week bye or keep today’s short games.

**7 teams, all present, 3 × 20.** Twelve play slots ÷ 7: five teams play two blocks, two play one. Prefer **2 plays each in 4 slots** (even tonight, round-robin in 3 weeks).

## Timed blocks vs reordering today’s games

| | Timed blocks (pilot) | Court-sticky halves (fallback) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What changes | Who you play stays put for ~20 min | Same ~3-min games and weekly round-robin; stay on a court longer |
| Transitions | 2 | Fewer court hops, still many opponent changes |
| Standings | Still count games inside the window | Unchanged |
| When to use | Goal is a real matchup window | Board wants fewer hops without changing who plays whom |

If the board is nervous: try court-sticky first (same Drive sheet, different order). If the goal is fewer transitions **and** a timed match vs the same opponent: 6-team timed blocks.

## How to present it

### Board (5 minutes)

One ask, then stop. Do not walk 5 / 7 / 8 unless someone asks about their roster.

| Beat | Say | Do not say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Tonight we rotate about every 3 minutes. Two courts is a lot of lineup and ref changes for little extra play. | The current format is broken. |
| Ask | Pilot one 6-team week: three 20-minute windows, same opponent each window, two rotates. Keep scoring games inside the block. | Switch the whole league next season. |
| Tradeoff | You will not play every team that night. Full cover takes 3 weeks. Playtime is already even (2 play, 1 ref). | Everyone still meets everyone tonight. |
| Other sizes | 5 / 7 / 8 only with a week bye (sit 1–2, run the even 4- or 6-team night). Do not pack those sizes into uneven 3-block weeks. | Every size can show up and play an even timed night. |
| Success | Captains report fewer dead minutes and a better match. If not, revert next week — it is a sheet, not a product change. | We need new software first. |

### Captains (Slack)

> Pilot week: three 20-minute matchups, both courts at once. Stay with that opponent the whole window. You ref one block. We still count games inside the window. You will not see every team tonight — that’s 3 weeks, same as covering a round-robin at this pace.

Send the 6-team sheet above, not the size matrix.

### Players (after the board says yes)

1. This week we are trying longer matchups: 20 minutes vs the same team, both courts running, then two quick rotates.
2. You play two blocks and ref one. How many games fit in 20 minutes is up to the court — the sheet only says who and how long.
3. You will not play every team tonight. Over three weeks you will. If it feels worse, we go back next week.

## Notes for whoever builds the sheet

- Hand a **one-off week**. Do not auto-generate this from today’s Drive templates. Those rules flag consecutive rematches and idle-while-reffing.
- Still **record 3-minute (or whatever) games inside the window** if standings need game counts.
- 2-hour gym cap: 5 long blocks do not fit (5 × 24 min + rotates ≈ 128 min). 2 or 3 long rounds do. Week-bye 4-team is 3 rounds.
- 20-minute blocks are the default. 24-minute is only on the 5-team week-bye (or 5-team packed) night, and still fits.
